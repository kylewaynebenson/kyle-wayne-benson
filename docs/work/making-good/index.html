Making Good

2018

After a successful pilot, BYUtv bought one season of Making Good. Cosmic Pictures came to me for a brand that could flex across the 12 episodes they would edit and deliver over the next year.

Me, Kyle
Brand Designer
Jack Mergist
Showrunner
Jeff Wiggins
Production Designer
Creative Direction
Jack Mergist, Making Good's showrunner, wanted it to feel like Saul Bass, Brady Bunch, Monster's Inc. I looked to 50s Betty Crocker cookbooks, and Alain Grée. We felt like these styles empathized Kirby's ability to connect person-to-person.
Guidelines
The deliverables included a 28 page pdf guidelines for editors, vendors, and caption or title designers. Throughout the process we talked a lot about making sure the in-house team at Cosmos didn't get stuck with a brand that needed a full time letterer or illustrator to implement it day in day out. With branding projects, my work ends where the real work begins. Nine months after the hand-off, I got to see the roll out of the brand across all its many touchpoints. It was fun to see all the ways their talented team put my tiny ideas into social media campaigns and full length episodes.
Logo Optical Sizes
Because the kit needed to span digital, print, and video applications, I created two optical sizes of the logo.
Typography
Throughout the process we talked a lot about making sure the in-house team at Cosmos didn't get stuck with a brand that needed a full time letterer or illustrator to implement it day in day out. This lead pretty naturally into the creation of some opinionated, bespoke fonts.
